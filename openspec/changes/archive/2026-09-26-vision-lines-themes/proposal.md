# Proposal

## Why

Framing themes as watch questions turned the watch into a SWOT of Europe's position and lost the Vision's broad lines. The watch should collect signals inside those lines, with the 2027 open questions as guidance, not replace them.

## Changes

- Themes are the Vision's broad lines again (the same 10 ids, so existing clusters carry over): a plain-words description, optional open questions, what to look for, keyword hints, a sweep query.
- Judgement prompts ask whether an item is a signal for a theme; direction is relative to the trend the Vision describes for it.
- Digest headings are the theme ids under "What moved in each theme".

- `vision-watch/weekly-digest`: section and heading names.
- `vision-watch/source-collection`: tips and sweep wording; sweep falls back to the description.
